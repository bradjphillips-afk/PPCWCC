How points are earned

Every match

Win +10 · Draw +4 (group games only) · each goal +2 · clean sheet +3 · yellow card −1 · red card −4

Knockouts: the team that advances gets the win (+10), plus +4 if it's won on penalties.

Group finish

1st +12 · 2nd +8 · 3rd & through +5 · 3rd & out +2 · bottom 0

Going deep (banked once)

Round of 16 +6 · Quarter-final +12 · Semi-final +20 · Final +30 · Champions +50

Golden gongs

If the winner is in one of your teams: Golden Boot +25 · Golden Glove +20 · Golden Ball +20

Bonuses

Beat a top-12 seed +8 · a shared team wins a match +6 (counts for both owners) · win all three group games +8

Mid-tournament window (after the groups)

One move each — Lifeboat: swap a knocked-out team for a surviving dark horse, +15 if it reaches the semis · Captain: one team scores double, but −12 if it loses its next knockout · or Pass.
