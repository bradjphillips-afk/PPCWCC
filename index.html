🤝 Shared teams bonus ▼

+6 to both owners each time a shared team wins a match.

Four teams are shared: Cape Verde, Ghana, Haiti, Iraq. If two players drew the same team, both benefit when it wins.

🆘 Rescue draft — knockout lifeline ▼

If you lost one or both of your draw teams in the group stage, you can rescue yourself by adopting surviving teams.

0 teams left → pick any 2 surviving teams
1 team left → pick any 1 surviving team

What you earn

50% of that team's knockout points — wins, goals, clean sheets, progression bonuses, and awards. All halved and rounded.

Multiple players can rescue the same team. Picks locked in before Round of 32. Shows in your breakdown as e.g. "France (rescue) +31".

🎯 The punt — round predictions ▼

Before each knockout round, every player tips the winner of every match in that round. Lock in before the first game kicks off.

Round of 32 — correct tip+8
Round of 16 — correct tip+12
Quarter-final — correct tip+18
Semi-final — correct tip+25
Final — correct tip+35

Wrong tip

0 pts — no penalty, just nothing.

Default pick

Didn't tip? You automatically get the lower-ranked team. You're cheering for the underdog whether you like it or not.

💥 The double bunger ▼

If you have a live team in the knockouts, declare a Double Bunger before any of their matches. Tell the commissioner before kickoff — once called, it's locked.

If they win

×2 on all points from that match.

If they lose

−12 penalty on top of normal scoring.

One Double Bunger per round. Fresh choice each round — no obligation to use it. Available from Round of 16 onwards.
